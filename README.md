<p align="center">The whole slayer loop in one RuneLite plugin. One card, one panel, one alert at a time.</p>

<!-- Screenshots live in docs/: task-card.png, task-complete.png, panel-task.png, panel-loot.png, hover.png -->

Slayer Suite reads the client, draws, and tells you things. It never attacks, prays, eats, drinks, moves or clicks for you, it does not say which prayer to use, and it shows nothing the game hides. It needs RuneLite's own Slayer plugin enabled.

## At a glance

- **Get a task.** Your own rates and skip advice in chat; the best spot for your account pinned on the world map.
- **Get there.** Chevrons on the floor from your feet to the spot, door to door, teleports weighed against the walk, fairy ring codes dialled for you.
- **Pack right.** At the bank: what the task needs, what you have, how much food and potion to take.
- **Fight.** One card with kills left, ETA, odds on the rare drops and your gear. One alert at a time. A superior you cannot miss. Your valuable drops marked on the floor.
- **Finish.** A completion card with the run's numbers and drops, then the route to the bank and back to your master.
- **Look back.** Your week, your levels, your monsters and your points on the Loot and Points tabs.

## Getting a task

- **Task choice helper.** Beside the master's task choice screen: your history, xp per kill, items you lack and the best spot for each task offered, with a pick.
- **Chat line.** Your own rates for the task when it is assigned, and a skip suggestion when it is well below your median, with the skip cost against your balance.
- **Spots ranked for your account.** Every spot is scored against your quests, diaries, levels, spellbook and the teleports you own, including your house, and against how long the trip takes from where you stand weighed against how long the task will run. A short task goes to the spot beside you; a long one to the cannon spot across the map.
- **Cannon and multicombat count only where they matter.** Nothing for crawling hands that die in a hit, everything for bloodvelds.
- **Pinned.** The best spot goes on the world map. The panel lists every way there, marked carried, in bank, not owned or needs a spellbook swap, with the time from here.

## Getting there

- **Chevrons from your feet.** A route searched on a map of every walkable tile with its doors, stairs, ladders, caves, trapdoors and the Agility shortcuts you can use, plus spirit trees, fairy rings, gnome gliders, boats and charter ships from the stop nearest you.
- **Teleports weighed in.** Items, spells, house portals and mounts you own are costed against the walk and named when clearly quicker. A walk is drawn whenever one exists.
- **Every transport marked.** Each door, stair and ride on the way is outlined with the option to click; a fairy ring with the code to dial.
- **Fairy ring dials.** On the ring's dial screen: the code, which dial to turn, which way, how many clicks, then the teleport button once it reads right.
- **Minimap and world map.** The next stretch dotted on the minimap; the whole plan on the world map, with where your teleport lands.
- **Cannon here.** A tile covering the most spawns, when you own a cannon and the spot allows one.
- **Learns the way in.** An entrance the tables lack is learned the first time you use it.
- **Between tasks.** When the assignment ends the chevrons carry on: to the nearest bank you can use when you carry fewer than three food or your inventory is nearly full, then back to the master you took the task from. Twenty minutes or until the next task. Off by a setting.

## At the bank

- **Readiness check.** The slayer helmet or the task's item, the finishing item, dragonfire or poison protection, food, prayer restores, cannonballs. Required in red, recommended in yellow, each marked when it sits in the bank you have open. Leaving with something required missing raises one alert.
- **Packing list.** How much food, how many prayer and other potions and antifire doses to take for the kills left, from your own use per kill on this task, with 15% spare, and whether it fits one trip.

## While you fight

- **Task card.** Kills left with a bar, ETA with the finish time, kills per hour, points and streak. The style to use, the superior, the finishing item. Where you are, with cannon, multi and safespot chips, or the pinned spot and the next step. A gear checklist: have, bring, missing.
- **Drop odds.** The monster's rarest drops with your kills since the last one and the chance you would have had it by now, from the wiki rates and your own kills.
- **Hover card.** Hover a task monster: Hitpoints, max hit, attack style, weakness, Slayer xp, the finishing item, poison and cannon warnings. Out of the way of the game's own hover text.
- **One alert at a time.** Low Hitpoints, Hitpoints falling fast (your own damage over the last fifteen seconds, carried on), venom, poison without a cure, low prayer, a boost wearing off, attacking the wrong thing, missing items, cannon empty or broken, the aggression timer, standing idle, supplies running out early, nearly done, superior, task done. Banner, RuneLite notification by severity, chat line, sound, a pause hotkey.
- **A superior you cannot miss.** Its outline and tile glow violet with SUPERIOR over its head until you attack it. An arrow at the edge of the view points at it when it is off screen. A dot pulses on the minimap. The alert and sound repeat every eight seconds until you engage it. A violet card when it spawns, a gold SUPERIOR DOWN card when it dies.
- **Money on the floor.** Your drops worth more than a threshold are outlined where they fell with the item, its value and the time until it vanishes. The card totals what lies there; an alert fires with thirty seconds left.
- **Attack guard.** Attack on monsters that do not count for your task is pushed off left-click or hidden.
- **Slaying mode.** Everything is on while you have a task and are in its area, fought a task monster in the last five minutes, or carry a slayer helmet, and quiet otherwise. You never switch the plugin off to do something else.

## When the task ends

- **Task complete card.** Kills, time, xp, loot, points and streak count up, with the drops you picked up as item tiles and the ones you left behind dimmer below. A Mortimer task shows its own count. The card holds while your mouse rests on it. The last few kills count down on screen.
- **Level up card.** The same card with the skill icon and everything slain and picked up since that skill's previous level.
- **Why a task was slow.** Minutes idle in the area, minutes away from it, and how your best run of the same task compared.
- **Task report.** One line per finished task, ready to paste: kills, time, xp, loot, best drop, points, streak and the slow-run breakdown. A button in the panel or `::ss report` copies it.

## Your numbers

- **Your week.** Kills, time, xp, loot and tasks for the last seven days against the seven before, kills per day as bars, the best drop of the week.
- **Slayer level.** The xp bar to the next level, virtual levels past 99, a maxed state at 200m. One card per level reached with its time, kills, loot and what was left on the floor.
- **This task.** Its drops, what you left behind, and its dry odds: every rare drop of the monsters you have been killing, how many you have seen, your kills since the last, the chance you would have one by now.
- **Monsters.** One page per monster you have fought: lifetime kills, time, xp and loot, your rates, best run, cannon comparison, superiors seen, every spot with your numbers there, the rare drops it has given you.
- **Spots that learn.** Kills, time, xp and loot per spot. The spot where you have done best gets the edge in the ranking.
- **Cannon.** Cannonballs loaded per task and what they cost, with your kills per hour with and without a cannon.
- **Points.** Your points, streak, the next bonus task, what a skip costs in tasks of points, the next milestone with hours to it, what you spent on skips, blocks and the shop, and the blocks worth buying for you from the master's roll weights against your xp per hour. A warning when you talk to Turael, Aya or Spria with a streak to lose.
- **History.** Per task, on your RuneScape profile: kills, time spent killing, Slayer xp, loot value, points per task, skips and blocks.

## Settings

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| Task card | Show, compact, keep while not slaying or without a task, number tiles, style row, place row, small print, drop odds, task choice helper, hover card. |
| Readiness check | Show at the bank, recommended items, food, prayer restores, cannonballs. |
| Alerts | Master switch, banner and its size, pause hotkey, thresholds (Hitpoints, falling fast, prayer, boost, idle, nearly done), a switch per alert, notifications by severity, chat line. |
| Task complete | The completion card and how long it stays, count-up and sparkles, countdown on the last kills, superior cards, superior highlight, level up card. |
| Sounds | Task complete, superior, critical, level up, volume. Your own `.wav` files in `.runelite/plugin-data/slayer-suite/sounds/` replace the bundled ones. |
| Attack guard | Guard Attack, deprioritise or hide, also without a task. |
| History and loot | Record history, chat line on a new task, skip threshold, floor marks and the value they start at. |
| Spots | Side panel, chat line with the best spot, pin the best spot, prefer cannon spots, avoid the Wilderness, hide locked spots. |
| Route | Minimap arrow, floor path, chevrons ahead, walk up to, after a task, route on the minimap and the world map, chevron colour (presets or your own), fairy ring dials. |

Slaying mode and the overlapping-plugins notice sit above the sections. When another enabled plugin does a job this one does (Slayer Task ETA, Scape Target, TaskJingle, Item Reminders, AFKing Toolkit alerts), a checklist at the top of the screen names each one and clears itself as you switch them off.

## Try the displays

Press the buttons at the bottom of the panel, or type in the chat box:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `::ss done` | The task complete card and sound. |
| `::ss superior` | The whole superior alert on the nearest monster: card, sound, warning, outline, arrow and minimap dot, for twenty-five seconds or until you attack it, then the down card. |
| `::ss level` | The level up card. |
| `::ss alert` | A critical alert. |
| `::ss supplies` | The supplies alert. |
| `::ss sound` | The task done sound. |
| `::ss report` | Copies the last task's one-line report. |
| `::ss vars` | Prints which game values the task is read from. |

Test events are not recorded in your history.

## Install

From the Plugin Hub: search for Slayer Suite.

From source, for sideloading in developer mode:

```
JAVA_HOME=/path/to/jdk11 ./gradlew jar
cp build/libs/slayer-suite-*.jar ~/.runelite/sideloaded-plugins/
```

`./gradlew sideload` does the copy for you.

## How it keeps the numbers honest

- **Task state** comes from the game's own tables, so the task is known at login with no gem check, for every master including Mortimer.
- **A drop counts as yours** only when the loot log attributed it to a task monster, the task's kill counter moved within four seconds of that death, and the item then appeared in your inventory while it still lay on the ground (two minutes for a tradeable drop, three for an untradeable one), within ten tiles of where it fell, on the same plane. Drops keep their task, so loot picked up after the task was handed in still counts for it. A confirmed drop that never reached your inventory is recorded as left behind. Grimy herbs into a carried herb sack and seeds into a seed box count as picked up when you step onto the drop.
- **Points** earned per task and the streak come from the game. Points leaving the balance are classified from the drop that precedes a task change: 30 is a skip, 100 a block, anything else a shop purchase. A task that vanishes with kills left, nothing paid and the streak intact went into task storage, and its kills, xp and loot are kept so it resumes where it left off.
- **The supply forecast** classifies what you carry: prayer restores become prayer points at your level, antifires become minutes, food and other potions are counted per kill. Antifire only counts on tasks that call for it. The forecast speaks up only when you will run short.

## Sounds

A chime when the task ends, an 8-bit encounter jingle when a superior spawns, a beep for critical alerts, a tick on the last kills. All of them are synthesised by `tools/make-sounds.py`; nothing is sampled from any game. Drop your own `task-done.wav`, `superior.wav`, `alert.wav` or `level-up.wav` into `.runelite/plugin-data/slayer-suite/sounds/` to replace them.

## The dataset

- `src/main/resources/com/slayersuite/data/monsters.json`: one record per assignment. Names and aliases, the NPC ids that count, every variant with its combat level, Slayer experience, Hitpoints, attack styles, poison flags and rare drop rates, the superiors, every location with its map regions and whether it is multicombat, cannonable or safespottable, the master ranges, requirements, and the hand-maintained item rules and style.
- `banks.tsv`, `masters.tsv` and `fairy-codes.tsv` beside it: every bank, every Slayer master and every fairy ring code with its tile, for the route between tasks and the dial helper.
- `src/main/resources/com/slayersuite/route/`: the walkable-tile map and transport tables of the Shortest Path project, taken at the commit in `SOURCE_COMMIT` and refreshed with `tools/sync-route-data.sh`. `transports/extra-transports.tsv` holds ways those tables lack. See `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

Everything from the wiki is built by `tools/build-dataset.py`:

```
python3 tools/build-dataset.py            # uses tools/cache, downloads only what is missing
python3 tools/build-dataset.py --refresh  # re-download everything
```

It reads every `Slayer task/<name>` page, follows each variant to its monster page for the infobox facts and drop tables, falls back to the monster page for core RuneLite task names that have no task page, reads the bank, master and fairy ring pages, and merges `tools/overrides.json`. It writes the data files, `DATA-ATTRIBUTION.md` (every page and revision used, with the licence) and `tools/report.txt` (coverage gaps).

Three hand-maintained files hold what the wiki does not state in a machine-readable way:

- `tools/overrides.json`: required items, finishing items, protection, style and notes per task, each entry dated once it has been checked against the wiki prose. `python3 tools/check-overrides.py <task-key>` prints the rules next to the wiki sentences they came from.
- `src/main/resources/com/slayersuite/data/travel.json`: per location, the quest or diary needed to get in and the teleports that reach it, with how far you still run afterwards; plus the generic list of teleports used between tasks.
- `src/main/resources/com/slayersuite/data/items.json`: the item groups those rules refer to and the item names that satisfy them. Matching is by normalised name, so charges, doses and imbues do not matter.

`tools/runelite-task-names.txt` lists the task and NPC names as the core Slayer plugin spells them; `tools/dump-core-task-names.sh` regenerates it from the client jar.

## Code map

| Package | Role |
|---|---|
| `task` | `TaskTracker` reads the task from the game's tables and the core service, counts kills, detects completion, points earned and spent, stored tasks and the superior standing. `GameTaskTable` is the table lookup. `SlayerMode` decides when the plugin is awake. |
| `data` | `SlayerDataset` loads the dataset and answers lookups by any spelling of a task name and by map region; `TravelPoints` holds the banks and masters. |
| `card` | The task card, the hover card, the task choice helper, the task complete and level up cards, and the superior highlight. |
| `readiness` | `ReadinessChecker` scans equipment, inventory and the open bank; `ReadinessOverlay` draws the bank list. |
| `alerts` | `AlertKind` is the priority table; `AlertEngine` evaluates the sources and fans out to the banner, the notifier, chat and sounds. |
| `guard` | `AttackGuard`, the menu-entry filter. |
| `advisor` | `LocationScorer` ranks spots through `AccountState`; `Advisor` keeps the pin, the next step and the travel options; `LoopGuide` carries the route on to the bank and the master between tasks; `GpsOverlay`, `PathOverlay` and `FairyDialOverlay` draw the arrow, the floor path and the dial helper; `HouseScanner` remembers your furniture; `SlayerSuitePanel` is the side panel. |
| `route` | `WorldMap` decodes the walkable-tile map, `TransportTable` the doors, stairs, caves and shortcuts, `Router` searches, `Planner` weighs teleports and rides, `RouteService` keeps the route fresh as you move. |
| `history` | `TaskHistory` keeps per-task stats, the assignment totals, the loot ledger, the per-day totals and level records; `PickupMatcher` turns inventory gains into confirmed drops; `FloorLootOverlay` marks your drops on the floor. |
| `trip` | `TripTracker` measures consumption per kill and projects kills until dry. |
| `sounds` | `SoundPlayer`, bundled sounds with per-file overrides. |
| `ui` | `Palette`, the one set of colours every in-game surface uses. |

## Roadmap

1. **Travel coverage.** `travel.json` has 46 locations; spots without an entry rank on their flags alone. Portal nexus destinations are not readable yet, so a nexus counts for its Xeric and Digsite additions alone.
2. **Looting bag.** Drops picked up into a looting bag never show in the inventory, so they read as left behind.
3. **Dataset in CI**, so wiki changes become a rebuild and a diff.
4. **Dataset gaps.** Some location rows have no map pins, a few boss and umbrella pages have no record, and 33 of the 149 task entries have been hand-reviewed so far. `tools/report.txt` lists them.
5. **Inventory Setups hand-off**: select the matching setup when that plugin is installed.
